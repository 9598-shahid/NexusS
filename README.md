# NEXUS — Credit Intelligence

> AI-Powered Corporate Credit Appraisal & Decision Support Platform

NEXUS is an intelligent corporate credit platform designed to help credit teams analyze corporate borrowers, identify potential risks, evaluate financial and operational information, and generate explainable credit recommendations.

The platform combines Artificial Intelligence, document intelligence, risk analysis, portfolio monitoring, audit tracking, and professional Credit Appraisal Memo (CAM) generation into one unified application.

## ✨ Features

- 🤖 AI-powered credit appraisal
- 🧠 Google Gemini AI integration
- 📄 PDF and document analysis
- 🔍 OCR-based text extraction
- 📊 Five-pillar credit risk analysis
- 📈 Interactive risk radar
- 💰 Recommended credit limit
- 📉 Indicative interest-rate recommendation
- 🚦 Credit approval/rejection recommendation
- 🚨 Early-warning risk identification
- 📝 AI-generated Credit Appraisal Memo
- 🖨️ Professional CAM PDF/print export
- 🗄️ SQLite-based appraisal storage
- 🔐 Audit and compliance logs
- 🏢 Corporate borrower management
- 📊 Portfolio dashboard
- 📋 Executive-level credit insights
- 🌐 Market and regulatory intelligence
